## This week's highlights

### Google's "Playground" and Unity's "Unity Spark": AI game creation in the browser

On October 7, Google released "Playground," an experimental platform where you can make games by describing them in text, then play and share them. At launch, it is open to people aged 18 and over in the United States. Creation features will roll out in stages depending on your Google AI subscription tier. Games can be kept private, shared by link, or published to a public gallery. On the same day, Unity announced "Unity Spark" in collaboration with Google. You can make and share games in the browser, and it connects directly to assets on the Asset Store. For now, it is accepting waitlist sign-ups. Neither the release date nor the pricing has been announced.

Neither announcement says whether these tools are available in Japan, or what happens to ownership and export of the games you make. For solo developers who build with coding agents, these are for now less a competitor than a growing number of entry points for non-programmers.

- Source: [Google blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/ai/playground-experimental-gaming-platform/), [Unity official blog](https://unity.com/blog/why-we-built-unity-spark)

### Removal requests pile up for works that recreate existing IP with AI

A browser version of "Fallout: New York," made with Claude Opus 5.5, was taken offline on October 2 after ZeniMax Media sent a notice of intellectual property infringement. The notice pointed to the use of trademarks and related materials. A project that decompiled and rebuilt *Call of Duty: MW2* with many AI agents also had its video removed after a complaint from the copyright holder. Regardless of whether AI was used, works based on existing IP are subject to removal.

- Source: [Automaton (in Japanese)](https://automaton-media.com/articles/newsjp/20261006-472053/)

### Game jams are split on generative AI rules

GMTK Game Jam 2027 (August 11–15, 2027) bans generative AI for art and audio, including thumbnails. Pixel Forge MEGA JAM (November 28 to December 8) bans generative AI entirely. Gamedev.js Jam 2027 (April 13–26) allows and encourages AI use. Check the rules before you submit.

- Source: [GMTK](https://itch.io/jam/gmtk-2027), [Gamedev.js](https://itch.io/jam/gamedevjs-2027), [Pixel Forge](https://itch.io/jam/pixel-forge-mega-jam)
