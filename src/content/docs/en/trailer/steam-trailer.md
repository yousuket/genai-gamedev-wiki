## Overview

On [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) store pages, trailers appear before screenshots and are one of the first elements visitors see.

This article summarizes information from [Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/) (Steam's developer management dashboard and documentation):

- Technical specifications for uploaded video files
- Display order, categories, and how micro trailers work
- Relationship with capsule images (the thumbnail images of games shown on the store)
- Points to check before publication

For structuring your trailer, see [Trailer Structure](/en/trailer/structure/). For export steps, see [Screen Capture and Editing Tools](/en/trailer/capture-and-editing/).

## Technical specifications

Specifications listed on [Steamworks: Trailers](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/trailer) (as of September 2026).

| Item | Specification |
|---|---|
| Resolution | Maximum 1920×1080 |
| Aspect ratio | 16:9 recommended (4:3 also accepted) |
| Frame rate | 30/29.97fps or 60/59.94fps |
| Bitrate | 5,000 kbps or higher bitrate |
| Container | .mov / .wmv / .mp4 |
| Video codec | H.264 recommended |
| Audio codec | AAC recommended. Audio is converted to stereo during processing |
| Sample rate | 44 kHz or 48 kHz |

For Adobe Media Encoder, Valve provides a preset: "H.264 Video (20Mbps) + AAC Stereo Audio (192kbps)".

### When conversion fails

The official FAQ lists two common causes of conversion failure:

- Non-standard resolution → Use 1920×1080 or 1280×720
- Unsupported audio sample rate → Use 44 kHz or 48 kHz

### Conversion wait time

- After uploading, conversion waits in a queue and can take <strong>several hours</strong>. Re-uploading moves your file to the back of the queue.
- <strong>You cannot release the game while the trailer is being converted</strong>. Avoid uploading immediately before the release date.

## Display on store page

### Display order and categories

- <strong>The first two valid trailers</strong> appear before screenshots; trailers 3 and beyond appear after screenshots. You can change the order by dragging in the management dashboard.
- Trailers can be assigned categories: "General / Cinematic," "Teaser," "Gameplay," or "Interview / Developer Diary." These categories appear next to the trailer name on the store.

[Steamworks](https://partner.steamgames.com/) recommends <strong>placing a gameplay-focused trailer first</strong>. From the player's perspective, showing "what you do in the game" with HUD visible makes it easier for them to judge whether it is what they are looking for.

### Assumptions about how it will be viewed

The Steamworks documentation states that trailers should be created with two assumptions in mind:

- In the Discovery Queue (which shows recommended games in sequence), viewers have <strong>less than 10 seconds</strong> to form an impression
- <strong>There is a possibility the video will be viewed without sound</strong>

The official Steamworks documentation does not specify whether videos auto-play when a store page opens, or whether audio is muted by default (as of September 2026). Since user settings and environment can vary, <strong>create a trailer where the content is clear even without sound, and present important information with text as well</strong> to be safe.

### 2025 player update

Valve updated the trailer player on July 30, 2025 ([Steam Trailer Player Upgrades](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/593110/view/500579723494031419)). Major changes include:

- Support for up to four quality levels: 360p, 480p, 720p, and 1080p, automatically switching based on connection and player size
- Seek bar preview: hovering over the seek bar shows a preview of where the video will jump to
- UI adapts to desktop, mobile, [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) Deck, and Big Picture mode
- Videos with any aspect ratio are displayed correctly through scaling, center-alignment, or letterboxing

The seek bar preview makes it easier for viewers to jump to specific scenes. If your trailer is structured so that each shot conveys content, it will be understood even if viewers skip around. While aspect ratio is now supported, 16:9 remains the recommended upload specification.

## Micro trailers

Micro trailers are short looping videos automatically generated by [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/). According to the official FAQ (as of September 2026), they are created as follows:

- Generated from <strong>the first trailer shown on the store page</strong>
- <strong>6 seconds</strong> long, made by cutting 1-second clips from 6 different positions in the video and joining them
- Developers cannot customize them

Micro trailers are used in previews that appear when users hover over capsule images and in other contexts. However, the official documentation does not list all the places where they appear (as of September 2026). This feature began as an experiment in Steam Labs ([Steam Labs - Micro Trailers](https://store.steampowered.com/labs/microtrailers)).

This means <strong>your lead trailer should remain coherent no matter where the 6-second clip is cut from</strong>. If logos, black screens, or text-only screens are shown for long periods, the micro trailer will not convey what the game is.

## Relationship with capsule images

Capsule images are static images registered separately from the trailer and appear in search results, sale lists, and elsewhere ([Steamworks: Graphical Assets](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets)).

| Image | Size (as of September 2026) |
|---|---|
| Header Capsule | 920×430 |
| Small Capsule | 462×174 |
| Main Capsule | 1232×706 |
| Vertical Capsule | 748×896 |

Also review the capsule image rules ([Graphical Asset Rules](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/store/assets/rules), effective September 1, 2022):

- Only game artwork, game title, and official subtitles are allowed on capsule images
- Review scores, awards, discount copy, or promotion of other products are not allowed
- Large updates or DLC announcements can be temporarily shown using "Artwork Override" (limited to 1 month)

In practice, <strong>it is important that people interested by the capsule image can confirm in the trailer that it is "the game I expected,"</strong> so the art style and atmosphere should match. If the capsule image shows lavish artwork while the trailer footage has a very different impression, viewers are more likely to feel disappointed.

### Trailer thumbnail

[Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/) automatically generates a poster image (600×380) and thumbnail (232×130) from each trailer. If you want to specify your own, <strong>upload a single frame from that video</strong> as a 1920×1080 .jpg or .png.

## Multilingual and regional settings

In "Advanced Settings," you can register language-specific trailers or restrict visibility by country. Set the base video to English and specify countries using ISO 3166-1 alpha-2 codes (e.g., `JP,US`).

## Pre-release checklist

- [ ] The lead trailer is in the "Gameplay" category and shows gameplay in the first few seconds
- [ ] It is clear what the game is even without sound
- [ ] The content makes sense even if any 6-second segment is cut out (no long logos or black screens)
- [ ] Exported at 1920×1080, 30fps or 60fps, H.264 + AAC (44.1 kHz or 48 kHz)
- [ ] Uploaded several days before the release date or event so conversion has time to complete
- [ ] Capsule image and trailer atmosphere match
- [ ] Video content matches the actual game ([see AI video generation use cases](/en/trailer/ai-video/))

For store page strategy to collect wishlists, see [How to Collect Wishlists](/en/monetization/wishlists/). For other Steam rules, see [Platform AI Policies](/en/legal/platform-policies/).

## How to use AI

- <strong>Automated specification checking:</strong> Pass the output of `ffprobe` to an LLM and ask it to verify that you meet Steamworks specs (resolution, fps, codec, sample rate). This reduces conversion failures.
- <strong>Preparing multilingual versions:</strong> Have the LLM translate title card text and subtitles to prepare for language-specific trailers.

```sh
# Check the resolution, fps, codec, and sample rate of the exported file
ffprobe -v error -show_entries stream=codec_name,width,height,r_frame_rate,sample_rate -of compact trailer.mp4
```
